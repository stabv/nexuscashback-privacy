Nexus Cashback: privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Nexus Cashback is a personal iPhone app that tells you which of your Singapore cashback credit cards to use for a purchase. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any bank.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data from the app.

What stays on your device

The cards you pick, your statement dates, your settings and the purchases you log are stored only on your iPhone (and in a storage area shared between the app and its widgets, which also stays on the device). iCloud sync is off. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Network

The app makes no network requests. The card information is built into each version of the app, so card updates arrive with app updates. If that ever changes, this page will be updated before the app sends anything.

Siri, Shortcuts and widgets

Siri phrases and widgets run on your device and read the same on-device data. Apple may process what you say to Siri under Apple's own privacy policy; the app does not receive recordings.

TestFlight

If you install a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple handles the invitation, your Apple ID email address and any feedback or crash reports you choose to send, under Apple's terms. The developer can see that feedback.

Not financial advice

Card rates and rules are summarised from public bank terms and can be out of date or wrong. The app gives estimates only. Always confirm with your bank.

Questions: contact the developer through the TestFlight feedback form.
